Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the process.

How long does processing take?

Most applications move from submission to dispatch within 3–4 weeks, depending on verification and appointment availability.

What documents do I need?

You'll need identity proof, address proof, date of birth proof, a photograph, and a signature scan.

Can I track my application without an account?

Yes — use the Track Application page with your Application ID at any time.

How do I change my appointment?

Open your dashboard and book a new slot — it will automatically replace your existing appointment.
